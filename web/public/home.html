Uniplanner

One calendar for all your university timetables.

Uniplanner is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Universita degli Studi di Torino (UniTO) or CINECA.

Uniplanner merges one or more public university timetables - the ones published on CINECA's "University Planner" calendars - into a single, personal calendar view. Pick exactly which courses (insegnamenti) from each timetable you want to see, arrange them into your own views, and browse them by day, week, month, or list.

The app works fully without an account: your sources and views are kept in your browser's local storage. Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to sync your sources, folders, and views across your devices.
